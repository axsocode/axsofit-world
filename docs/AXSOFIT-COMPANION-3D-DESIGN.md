# AXSOFIT COMPANION — Character Design Brief

## Character identity
A new friendly fitness-game companion. It is not the player's avatar and must not replace the current humanoid player model.

## Visual language
- Stylized 3D animated-film aesthetic, warm and approachable.
- Cartoon body proportions: head-to-body ratio approximately 1:3.2 to 1:3.6.
- Large readable eyes, soft facial planes, expressive eyebrows and a clear mouth silhouette.
- Compact athletic build rather than anatomical realism.
- Black/charcoal training outfit with restrained AXSOFIT red accents and a small neutral geometric chest mark.
- Warm skin shading, dark swept hair, clean sports shoes.
- No direct imitation of copyrighted characters or studio-specific character designs.

## Personality through performance
- Greeting: friendly smile, eye contact, small wave.
- Explaining: attentive eyes, small nods, measured hand gestures.
- Motivating: confident smile, open posture, one encouraging gesture.
- Listening: relaxed shoulders, slight head tilt, soft expression.
- Celebrating: broad smile, raised arms, brief bounce.
- Empathy: relaxed posture, softened eyebrows, no exaggerated sadness.

## MVP scope
1. Neutral idle with subtle breathing and natural blink.
2. Five core expressions plus surprised.
3. Five action clips: greet, explain, motivate, celebrate, listen.
4. State-driven animation API; no direct coupling to chat UI.
5. GLB delivery and mobile performance review before production integration.

## Non-goals for this phase
- Replacing the multiplayer player avatar.
- Facial motion capture or live camera tracking.
- Voice/lip-sync.
- Cloud AI, mood diagnosis or health recommendations.
- Persistent personal data storage.
